# Fuse 1.10.1 – Hungarian Windows edition 1 (v1.10.1-hu.1)

The second release of this fork of Fuse, the ZX Spectrum emulator, for
Windows with its native Windows interface, in Hungarian or English – now
based on Fuse 1.10.1. The main news are the WASAPI sound output and a
reworked AY music player.

## Highlights

- **WASAPI sound** – `fuse.exe` now uses the sound system of today's
  Windows: lower latency, and the sound is fed from a thread of its own. When
  the Windows default output is changed, or the device in use is unplugged,
  the sound moves to the new default device without restarting Fuse.
  `fuse-directsound.exe` is the same program with DirectSound, as a fallback;
  both use the same settings.
- **AY player apart from the emulated machine** – songs run on a Z80 of
  their own with an AY sound generator of their own. The running program is
  no longer stopped or replaced: it goes on, and its sound is heard together
  with the music. With `fuse.exe` the music plays on during menus, dialog
  boxes and window moves.
- **Song lengths measured in the background** while the song plays, in well
  under a second, also for a whole playlist; seeking is instant in both
  directions.
- **Pro Tracker 3 modules** (`.pt3`, also from Vortex Tracker II) can be
  played too.
- **Volume of each AY channel** – a slider beside each level meter in the
  player, also in *Options > Sound*.
- **New stereo modes BCA and CAB**; the modes are listed as ABC, ACB, BAC,
  BCA, CBA, CAB, Mono and Custom (*None* is now called *Mono*).

The full list of changes is in
[CHANGELOG-fork.md](https://github.com/Viktor318/fuse/blob/hungarian-windows/CHANGELOG-fork.md).

## Installation

1. Download `Fuse-1.10.1-hu.1-windows.zip` below.
2. Unpack it into a folder of your choice (or over the previous release).
3. Start `fuse.exe` – or `fuse-directsound.exe` if the sound doesn't work
   well with WASAPI on your computer. Nothing has to be installed.

## Good to know

- Settings and the other files Fuse keeps are in **`%APPDATA%\Fuse`** (type it
  into the address bar of Explorer); the settings of the previous release are
  used as they are.
- The measured song lengths are kept. Songs whose length the previous release
  gave up on (it stopped after 15 seconds) are measured again the next time
  they are played.
- Pause in the AY player stops the music only; *Machine > Pause* pauses the
  emulated machine as before.
- With `fuse-directsound.exe` the music is mixed into the sound of the
  emulator, so it stops while a dialog box is open or the machine is paused.
